# V5 hero concepts (1 Nov 2026)

Brief: "a beautifully designed analytical instrument". Complexity → Structure → Insight → Creation. Every animation must show something you can learn. Three working concepts, all synthetic data generated and computed in the browser. Canvas 2D with a small orbit camera (lib.js); no libraries.

- **A · Perspective** (v5-a): 1,400 customers × 3 behaviours. One cloud from the recorded angle; k-means (6 starts, every iteration shown); the camera rolls to the angle that separates the groups, with a histogram along that direction; the direction becomes a rule. Computed: rule accuracy on 560 unseen customers 99.8% vs best single column ~80%; the direction's coefficients; between/within separation; segment profiles in real units. Interactions: drag to orbit, add a customer, jump to any stage.
- **B · Relationships** (v5-b): 36 stores × 104 weeks. A wall of small charts and the 36×36 correlation matrix on the floor. Average-linkage clustering on 1 − r reorders the matrix into blocks; charts regroup, then stack to show each group's shape (named from the data: summer peak / holiday spike / steady growth); the store that breaks from its group is found by change-point on its residual vs the group (week 70, −38%); groups become a 12-week forecast and a monitor. Interactions: hover any chart for its group and correlation, drag to orbit.
- **C · Landscape** (v5-c): 360 weeks of discount × ad spend → profit. Points with stems; a least-squares quadratic surface rises from flat (R² shown); top-down contours locate the peak analytically, and the surface recolours by prediction uncertainty: the peak sits where there are 6 weeks of evidence vs 73 at the usual spot; then a planner: hover the floor for a forecast ± 95% range and cross-section slices. Interactions: drag to orbit, hover to plan, "Go to the peak".
